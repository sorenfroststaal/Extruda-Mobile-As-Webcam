# 0.17.0 — Phone viewfinder and phone-side resolution

- Phone: after pairing, the page becomes a fullscreen viewfinder with five large controls: flip camera, microphone, hide picture, dim screen and settings.
- The pairing screen shows the code large and centered with a short status line.
- Hide picture sends a black image to the PC and stays hidden when you flip the camera.
- Dim screen covers the phone display with black while keeping the page awake. Tap to show it again.
- The settings sheet holds resolution, microphone, recording, connection health and Disconnect.
- Windows: the receiver accepts resolution requests from the phone and applies the same rules as the PC selector (no change while recording or while another change is running). The phone shows when a request is refused.
- Older receivers never get the new message and keep choosing resolution on the PC. Install this Windows update to unlock the phone selector; refreshing the phone page does not update the desktop receiver.
- New 26A icons: camera switch, microphone, microphone off, hide picture.

Validation: automated camera-start tests cover the capability gating, PC-side apply/refuse, and hidden picture across camera switches. The viewfinder was checked in a mobile-size browser. Physical iPhone/Android and meeting-app validation remains manual.
